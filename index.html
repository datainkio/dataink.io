DOC 01 / Manifesto
Russell Lebo / Selected Works Archive2008-2026

Design for a more just, informed, and engaged human experience

I am an experience designer and creative technologist

I deliver UX strategy, interaction design, and information architecture for organizations across healthcare, civic tech, human rights, museums, cultural institutions, global brands, and education.

Equitable access to insight

The work that means the most to me is work that helps people to find answers in complex bodies of data.

Make it invisible

People say the best interface is invisible. I disagree. An interface - whether it is visual, programmatic, or physical - can be an opportunity for beauty. What should be invisible is the business logic.

    A Human Face for the AIDS Epidemic

    The Emmy and Webby-winning website "Live Hope Love" used a blend of poetry, photography, video interviews, and music to create a deeply human and emotive online experience.

  • USDA, Bixal

    Digital Transformation for Public Health

    The project for USDA aimed to revamp the digital presence for the agency's food safety and inspection service, crucial for safeguarding public health by protecting the food supply. This involved creating a user-friendly and informative website to build public trust and aid policy formulation.

    Deliverables
    Agile Design, Card Sorting, Content Strategy, Information Architecture, Research Planning, Service Design, Stakeholder Interviews, Usability Testing
    View

  • Columbia Sportswear, Transistor Studios

    Beautiful People Doing Stupid Things

    As part of the largest and most integrated global marketing campaign in Columbia's history, the Omni-Heat design emphasized cinematic seamlessness as users navigated through product shots and videos of incredibly attractive people engaging in humorous, exaggerated activities.

    Deliverables
    Bilingual Content Strategy, Development, Wireframing
    View

  • IBM Interactive

    A Trusted Advisor for Doctors and Caregivers

    IBM's CarePlex was an innovative healthcare platform utilizing Watson’s artificial intelligence to enhance the medical care process for clinicians, patients, and caregivers. The project, a collaboration between IBM and Boston Children’s Hospital, aimed to integrate cognitive computing into healthcare to provide dynamic medical records and personalized care recommendations.

    Deliverables
    Information Visualization, Interaction Design, Product Design
    View

  • IBM Interactive

    Smart Decisions, Smart Cities

    CityForward was an innovative web platform developed by IBM as part of its Smarter Planet Initiative. Designed to serve city officials, researchers, academics, and engaged citizens, CityForward offered a comprehensive view of urban life across 250 major cities globally. The platform aimed to facilitate data-driven decision-making and foster a deeper understanding of urban trends.

    Deliverables
    Development, Graphic Design, Interaction Design
    View

  • ICA Boston, Bluecadet

    A Museum that Talks with its Visitors

    Bluecadet and the Institute of Contemporary Art (ICA) Boston took an innovative approach to enhance visitor engagement and accessibility to the museum’s art collections through interactive kiosks. The project sought to transform the museum experience by integrating technology into the gallery-education space, enabling rich dialogues between the museum, its artists, and its visitors.

    Deliverables
    Development, Interaction Design
