organisms

Process section

A heading and two paragraphs of process copy, with optional decorative scenes that motion fills in.

Specimens

Params

ParamTypeDescription
idstringSection id. Defaults to process.
copyobjectheading (defaults to “Process”) and body (optional paragraph, omitted when empty).
headingIdstringId for the heading and aria-labelledby. Defaults to the section id.
uiComponentsbooleanWhen true, renders the UI-components loop stage above the paragraphs. Defaults to false.

Fixed design: takes no styling params.
