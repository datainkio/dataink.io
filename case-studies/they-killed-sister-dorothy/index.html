They Killed Sister Dorothy

Bluecadet created the companion website for a documentary film about Sister Dorothy Stang, an environmental and social activist murdered in the Amazon rainforest. The website aimed to complement the film's narrative and deepen public awareness of the environmental and social issues it presented.

Overview

01 / Problem
"They Killed Sister Dorothy" documents the 2005 murder of an American nun who defended the Amazon, and the trials that followed. Its companion site had to hold the horror of the killing beside the forest she died for, and move visitors to act.
02 / Design Challenge
As Bluecadet's Lead Developer, Russ designed the interactions and built the site, working from the visual designers' mockups.
03 / Approach
A seven-tab cast list gives the killers, the rancher and the defense the same weight as the nun and her brother. Backgrounds alternate between the forest and the crime, and the navigation ends with "What can you do?"
04 / Outcome
The site won HOW and Communication Arts honors, per the case study. The film and site "helped keep international attention" on the case until the 2010 convictions, a contribution no one can measure.

Process + Evidence Plates

    • Fig 01 / Information Architecture

    Decision: Give every party in the case its own tab, the accused included

    Result: Visitors weighed the whole story, and the site's advocacy gained credibility

    • Fig 02 / Graphic Design

    Decision: Alternate backgrounds between the forest and the crime

    Result: Neither the violence nor the rainforest overwhelmed the other

    • Fig 03 / Content Strategy

    Decision: End the navigation with "What can you do?"

    Result: Viewers left with a way to act, not just a story

    • Fig 04 / Content Strategy

    Decision: State the facts of the killing plainly, without sensationalizing

    Result: The site treated a real death with the gravity it deserved

    • Fig 05 / Content Strategy

    Decision: Give the family's voice its own place in the story

    Result: The personal loss stayed visible alongside the legal case
