A Human Face for the AIDS Epidemic

The Emmy and Webby-winning website "Live Hope Love" used a blend of poetry, photography, video interviews, and music to create a deeply human and emotive online experience.

Overview

01 / Problem
For the Pulitzer Center, poet Kwame Dawes and photographer Joshua Cogan documented people living with HIV in Jamaica, at a time when such subjects were usually anonymized. The site had to let the poems, images and voices speak without overwhelming the viewer.
02 / Design Challenge
As Bluecadet's Lead Developer, Russ designed the interactions and built the site, working from the visual designers' mockups.
03 / Approach
The 22 poems are the navigation, each paired with a photograph. Vital Voices names and portrays the people Dawes wrote about, with their own words grouped by the topics they chose.
04 / Outcome
Captures of the finished site survive. Per the case study it won a News & Documentary Emmy and a Webby People's Voice Award, which measure reception, not impact.

Process + Evidence Plates

    • Fig 01 / Experience Design

    Decision: Respect & Amplify the Content

    Result: A design strategy in which the poetry, images, and stories speak for themselves without overwhelming the viewer.

    • Fig 02 / Content Strategy

    Decision: Photography's Role

    Result: Amplify the sentiment of the poems without losing focus on the poetry itself.

    • Fig 03 / Content Strategy

    Decision: Voices of the People

    Result: The production team returned to Jamaica to record the voices of individuals featured in Dawes’ poems, lending authenticity and depth to the narrative.

    • Fig 04 / Interaction Design

    Decision: Make the 22 poems the navigation, each with equal weight

    Result: The source work stayed whole instead of being recategorized as journalism

    • Fig 05 / Accessibility Standards & Best Practices

    Decision: Keep interviews intimate, with clear captions and controls

    Result: Accessible, respectful, and easy to follow

    • Fig 06 / Content Strategy

    Decision: Group each person's words under the topics they chose to speak about

    Result: Subjects set the terms of their own story

    • Fig 07 / Design Systems

    Decision: Present poems, photographs, video and music in one minimal frame

    Result: The interface never competed with the work
